How it started

It crept up on me. Every step felt small enough to ignore. I knew something was wrong long before I was willing to admit it.

I once won €68,000. That became the start of years of chasing that feeling. I gambled the win away and more, and after that I was mostly trying to win back what I had lost. That never works.

I once gambled away my whole salary in one evening. I told people everything was fine. That it was just a distraction. When I could not come along to something, I had a reason. The real reason was that there was no money.

By the end I was more than €40,000 in debt. On credit cards, through personal loans, and borrowed from people close to me.

How I stopped

I did not stop in one go. After one of my first attempts I relapsed. That happens to a lot of people, and it does not mean it will never work.

I had a few months of professional help. What made the difference after that was routine. I gambled mostly in the evenings and at weekends, when there was no work. So those were exactly the hours I filled: fixed things at fixed times, exercise, reading about self-development, and learning to let go of the thought that one bet would fix everything.

The first weeks were hard. The urges and the emptiness. Triggers I did not see coming, and waking up at night with one thought. After a few weeks it got better, and my sleep came back.

I have now been gambling-free for more than two years.

Paying off the debt

The debt did not disappear when the gambling stopped. It took me almost two years. I lived on a fixed amount every month, and everything above that went to the debt. Every extra euro too.

The hardest part was working hard without seeing anything back straight away. And not having anything to show the people around me. Nobody sees you paying off a debt. You do it for yourself.

The debt is now fully paid off. The whole story is in how I paid off my gambling debt.

Why Afterbetting exists

When I stopped, I was looking for something for the hours in between. A helpline is not something you call every evening. A forum is mostly other people's stories. A spreadsheet does not stop you on a Friday night.

Afterbetting is what I wish I had had. A day counter. Small habits for the hours you used to gamble. A journal. An overview of your money, so you see what you are no longer losing. And a button for when the urge hits, at night too.

I did not use the app myself when I stopped, because it did not exist yet. I used the same methods. The app grew out of what I, and the people around me, were missing back then.

How we work

  • No investors, no ads. Not on the site and not in the app. Afterbetting only earns from the Plus subscription.
  • Free stays free. The day counter, the Recovery kit and Help now are always free.
  • Your data is yours. Only you see your journal and your numbers. We sell nothing, and you can export or delete everything.
  • Not a medical service. Afterbetting does not replace treatment. It works alongside it, for the evenings, the weekends and the moments you are on your own.
